Evidence

    What to do

    ScamSpecter is a transparent rule-based check. It cannot see the real website behind a link and it cannot guarantee safety. A "Low risk" result means no strong scam signals were found — stay alert anyway.

    How it works

    1. URL rules — shorteners, raw IP addresses, typosquats of Indonesian brands (BCA, BRI, DANA, Shopee, …), suspicious domain endings, .apk downloads, missing HTTPS.
    2. Message patterns — Indonesian scam language: prize lures, OTP/PIN requests, urgency and threats, "admin fee" demands.
    3. Screenshot OCR — text is read from your screenshot on your device, then checked with the same rules.
    4. Offline-first — all scoring runs locally in your browser. No account, no upload, no tracking.
