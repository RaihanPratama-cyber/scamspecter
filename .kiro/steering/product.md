# Product steering — ScamSpecter

- **One core feature only**: paste a link/message → risk score + evidence + advice. Everything else is cut.
- **User**: Indonesians who get suspicious links on WhatsApp, SMS or email and cannot tell a real bank/courier notice from a fake one.
- **Tone**: a serious security tool wearing a Halloween costume. The ghost motif is decoration; the result panel is plain, high-contrast and trustworthy.
- **Honesty rule**: never claim certainty. The engine is heuristic. "Low risk" means "no strong signals found", not "safe". Say so on the page, in the README and in the Devpost draft.
- **Privacy rule**: nothing leaves the device. No account, no upload, no analytics, no tracking. OCR text stays in the browser.
- **Demo rule**: the demo must run 10 times in a row without failing. That is why the 6 sample cases exist and why scoring needs no network.
